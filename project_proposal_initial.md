#### SER541: Project Proposal
#### Title: Recognizing Differences in Purchase Behavior among E-Commerce Demographics
#### Anonymous
#### Date: 01/28/2026

Keywords:

E-commerce analytics, Purchasing behavior analysis, Consumer segmentation 

Description: 

In order to identify connections between demographics, buying habits, and transaction feratures, this project examines the purchasing behavior of online shoppers. 3,900 customers' demographics, purchase preferencs, and transactional information are included in the dataset.
There are some research questions: Which demographic variables indicate how much a purchase will cost? What unique consumer groups are there according to their purchasing habits? What differences exist between groups in the use of discounts? How do customer value and shopping frequency relate to each other?
It focuses on knowledge discovery-finding behavioral insights.

Intellectual Merit: 

Using data from 2025, this research project investigates the relationship between e-commerce purchase habits and demographics. While consumer segmentation is known, this analysis looks at whether new trends in post-pandemic digital commerce have evolved or whether existing methods are still applicable. 
The study may reveal unanticipated connections between discount-seeking behavior and demographic characteristics, or it may find new client segments outside of traditional categories. 

Data Sourcing: 

Mendeley Data (DOI:10.17632/xv99b2mtfk.1), published in January 2025 by Hariharan S. and licensed under CC by 4.0, is the source of dataset, which includes 3,900 customer records with transactional details, buying preferences, and demographics.

Retrieval: After agreeing to the license terms, a CSV or Excel file can be downloaded directly from the Mendeley Data platform. The majority of variables in the data structure are quantitative; during munging, categorical variables like gender, location, and product categories need to be encoded. 
Preprocessing requirements will be ascertained, missing values or outliers will be found, and completeness will be evaluated.
